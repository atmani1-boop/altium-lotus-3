🌿 How to play
Move: W A S D / Z Q S D / arrows · Run: Shift · Jump / swim: Space
Look around: drag with the mouse · Zoom: mouse wheel
Build: B, then pick a piece with 1–0 · Place: click · Rotate: R · Remove: right-click or X
Walls, doors and windows snap to the floor edges. Roofs go on top of the walls when you aim inside the room.
